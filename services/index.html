Water Damage and Mold Services in Indio, CA

Six services, each answering a different question, from stopping the water to rebuilding the room.

☎ Call (760) 769-1159 24/7

A water loss moves through stages, and each stage has its own service. Knowing which one you need helps you describe the problem on the phone and understand the estimate you receive.

Start with the situation you are in. If water is standing in the building, you need extraction. If it has stopped but everything is wet, you need drying. Once the structure is dry, you need repair. Mold sits at the end of that chain, because it only grows where moisture lingers.

Which service answers which question

ServiceThe question it answersTypical starting point
Emergency water removalHow do I get standing water out right now?Burst pipes, failed appliances, overflows
Water mitigationHow do I stop the damage from spreading in the first days?Wet walls, floors and ceilings after the source is stopped
Water damage cleanupHow do I remove ruined materials and sanitize?Contaminated water, soaked carpet and debris
Flood damage restorationHow do I recover from storm runoff or sewage?Storm water, groundwater and Category 3 water
Water damage repairHow do I rebuild once everything is dry?Drywall, insulation, flooring and paint
Mold remediationHow do I deal with mold that moisture left behind?Visible or suspected mold after a leak

Mold remediation is a secondary service and usually follows a moisture problem.

Unsure which service matches what you see? Describe it on the phone and the dispatcher will point you to the right one.
