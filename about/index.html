About Indio Water Damage Pros

A dispatch line for water damage and mold problems in Indio, California and the nearby Coachella Valley towns, open at every hour.

☎ Call (760) 769-1159 24/7

Indio Water Damage Pros answers calls from people with water where it should not be. We listen to what happened, explain the first steps to take while help is on the way and send a licensed and insured restoration professional to the property.

This page explains how that works in plain terms, including the limits of what we do. A short, honest description of a service is more useful than a list of claims, so we have kept this one specific.

How the dispatch line works

Every call goes to a dispatcher who asks the same few questions: where is the water coming from, has it been stopped, what is wet and what is the address. The answers decide what you are told to do immediately and which kind of crew is needed. A supply line failure in a kitchen is a different job from a sewer backup, and the dispatcher treats them differently.

The dispatcher then arranges a crew and tells you what to expect. Crews respond the same day when one is free, and we do not promise an arrival time we cannot control. Before work starts, the crew lead explains the scope and how the price is set, so there are no surprises after the equipment is running.

What licensed and insured means here

The line dispatches to restoration professionals, not to one fixed crew. The professional who arrives is licensed and insured, which is the standard we ask of everyone we send. We do not publish license numbers, insurer names or individual company names on this site.

You do not have to take anyone's word for it. California requires a contractor license for work valued at $1,000 or more in combined labor and materials, and the Contractors State License Board lets you check a license on its website. Ask the person who arrives to show you their license and proof of insurance before work begins.

Standards we point homeowners toward

The restoration industry publishes two standards that matter to homeowners. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company you are considering whether its crews work to them. A clear answer is a good sign, and a vague one is worth noticing.

What we do not do

Being clear about limits helps you call the right trade the first time.

  • We do not waterproof foundations or basements. Restoration repairs damage that already happened, while waterproofing aims to keep water out later.
  • We do not repair plumbing. A plumber fixes the broken pipe, valve or water heater, and the crew deals with the water it released.
  • We do not patch roofs. If a roof leak started the loss, a roofer stops the source and the restoration crew dries and repairs the interior.
  • We do not promise how your insurer will respond. We document the loss, and your policy and your insurer decide coverage.
  • We do not make health claims about mold. Health questions belong to a physician.
